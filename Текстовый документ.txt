# Workspaces

## Overview

FinCore is a multi-tenant SaaS platform.

Every user has **one Account**.

An Account may belong to one or many **Workspaces**.

A Workspace represents an isolated business context where the user performs a specific role.

The application behavior depends on the selected Workspace.

---

## Workspace Types

```text
Platform Workspace

Company Workspace

Bank Workspace

Customer Workspace
```

Each workspace has its own:

- navigation
- permissions
- theme
- enabled modules
- dashboard
- business logic

---

## User Account

```text
Account

↓

Workspace

↓

Role

↓

Permissions
```

The account is used only for authentication.

Business permissions are determined by the selected Workspace.

↓

---

## Workspace Selection

The Workspace selection screen appears:

- after the first login
- if the user has multiple workspaces
- when the user explicitly switches workspace

The interface is similar to Netflix profile selection.

Example:

```text
Choose Workspace

🏢 My Factory Ltd

Company Owner

-------------------

🏦 Business Bank

Bank Manager

-------------------

💳 Personal Banking

Customer
```

---

## Remember Last Workspace

After selecting a Workspace the platform stores it.

On the next login the application automatically opens the last active Workspace.

The user may switch Workspace at any time.

## Design Principles

- One Account
- Multiple Workspaces
- Workspace defines business context
- Role exists only inside Workspace
- Permissions belong to Workspace
- Navigation belongs to Workspace
- Theme belongs to Workspace
- Enabled Modules belong to Workspace
