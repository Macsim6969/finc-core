# Event Storming - Big Picture

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document describes the major business events occurring in the FinCore platform.

The purpose is to understand:

- how the business works;
- where business processes begin and end;
- how domains communicate;
- which domains own the data;
- where future Bounded Contexts should be separated.

This document intentionally ignores technical implementation.

Only business events are described.

# Event Storming Principles

Every business process starts with a Command.

Commands produce Domain Events.

Events trigger Policies.

Policies execute new Commands.

Read Models are updated asynchronously whenever possible.

Business logic belongs inside Business Domains.

Cross-domain communication should happen through events instead of direct dependencies whenever appropriate.
